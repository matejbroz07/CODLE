What you can do on Codle

Follow a learning path

Courses in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, split into units and short lessons. Each lesson builds on the one before it.

Write real code

Every task opens in a code editor with a live preview. Codle checks your code step by step and tells you what is still missing.

Start at your level

A short placement quiz when you sign up finds out what you already know, so you can skip the units you have mastered.

Test yourself

Unit quizzes and final exams show whether the knowledge stuck before you move on.

Play daily games

Guess the coding word of the day, sort programming milestones by age and read a short story from the history of computing.

Stay motivated

Earn XP, keep your daily streak, collect badges and see how you rank on the weekly leaderboard.

How it works

  1. Pick a language. Choose HTML, CSS or JavaScript.
  2. Take the placement quiz. Ten questions that adapt to your answers.
  3. Create your account. Sign up with your e-mail, or with Google or GitHub.
  4. Learn every day. Your progress is saved, so you can continue on any device.

Signing in with Google or GitHub

You can sign up and log in with your Google or GitHub account instead of confirming your e-mail with a code. Codle uses that sign-in only to identify you. It receives your account ID, your verified e-mail address and your name, which is used to suggest a username. Codle does not read your e-mails, files, repositories or anything else, and it never posts on your behalf.
