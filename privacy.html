Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Codle collects when you use app.codle.eu, why we collect it, who else handles it and what you can do about it. We collect only what is needed to run your account and keep the service safe. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

1. Who is responsible

Codle is run by a private individual based in the Czech Republic, who is the controller of your personal data. For anything about your data, write to info@codle.eu.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Username, e-mail address and your password. The password is stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form.
  • Time zone, daily XP goal and whether your profile is public.
  • Optional profile details you add yourself: a bio and a profile picture. Pictures are re-encoded on our server, which removes hidden metadata such as location.
  • Your earlier usernames, kept for 90 days so nobody else can take over a name you just gave up.

Your learning

  • Answers and result of the placement quiz you take when you sign up.
  • Lesson progress, quiz scores, the code you write in the editor, XP, streaks and badges.
  • Results of the practice games (Wordle, Older or younger, Daily story).
  • Your settings for the editor, the app theme and the Curly mascot.

Signing in

  • One record per logged-in device: a hashed session token, a short device label such as "Chrome on macOS", and when the session started and was last used.
  • One-time 6-digit codes we e-mail to you. They are stored only as a hash and expire after 15 minutes.
  • If you sign in with Google or GitHub: your account ID with that provider and your verified e-mail address. We use your name or GitHub username only to suggest a Codle username. We do not store access tokens from these providers and we do not read anything else from your Google or GitHub account.
  • Counters that limit how often someone can try to log in or request codes. They are keyed by a hash of the e-mail address, not by your IP address, and are deleted within a day.

Technical logs

Our web server keeps access logs with the IP address, time, requested address and browser type of each request. We use them only to find errors and stop attacks, and they are deleted after 14 days. The Codle application itself does not store IP addresses.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To provide Codle to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract): your account, your learning progress, the leaderboard and your profile.
  • To keep Codle secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest): technical logs, login limits and the invisible check against automated sign-ups.
  • To send service e-mails (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): verification codes, password reset codes and notices when your password or e-mail changes. We do not send newsletters or marketing.

4. What other learners see

Your username, profile picture and level appear on the leaderboard for everyone who is logged in. If your profile is public, which is the default, other learners can also see your bio, statistics and badges. You can make your profile private in Profile → Settings at any time. Your e-mail address is never shown to other learners.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account and learning data: for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, all of its data, including your profile picture, is deleted from our database right away.
  • Sessions: until they expire after 30 days or you log out. Codes: 15 minutes, removed within a day. Login limit counters: up to a day. Server logs: 14 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Most of this you can do yourself in Profile → Settings:

  • Download my data gives you everything we store about you as a JSON file.
  • You can change your username, e-mail, password, bio, picture and visibility there.
  • Delete account removes your account and all its data for good.

For anything else, write to info@codle.eu. We reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

9. Children

Codle is suitable for young learners. If you are under 15, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account.

10. Security

Codle is served only over HTTPS. Passwords, codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes. Changing your password signs you out on all other devices, and we e-mail you when your password or e-mail address changes.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will let you know in the app or by e-mail before it takes effect.
